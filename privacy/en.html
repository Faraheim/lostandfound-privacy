Privacy Policy

Lost and Found – personal app for items left behind

Last updated: 15 September 2026 · Version 1.1

Norsk versjon

1. Controller

The developer of Lost and Found (Nummis). Contact: bomberomapp@gmail.com.

2. What the app does

You register items left at your place: photo, description and the owner’s phone number. The app helps you send SMS reminders (via your phone’s SMS app, and optionally via a server SMS provider if enabled).

3. Data we process

  • Photos of the item — stored on a server and may be shared via a link in SMS.
  • Phone numbers — often belonging to another person — stored for reminders.
  • Descriptions of the find.
  • Contacts: used on-device to pick a number. We do not upload your entire address book.
  • Notifications: device token if you allow push.
  • SMS content: sent to the recipient; reminders include the option to reply STOPP / STOP.

4. Purpose

Help return forgotten items through agreed reminders.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. SMS recipients receive the text and image link. If server SMS is enabled, an SMS provider (e.g. Sveve) may process the number and message. The hosting provider processes technical server data.

6. Retention and deletion

  • When you mark an item returned, the associated photo is deleted.
  • When you stop reminders in the app (for example after the recipient replies STOPP — the reply arrives in your SMS inbox; the app does not read inbound SMS automatically), status is updated.
  • You can request deletion by email.

7. Rights

Access, correction and deletion via email. Complaint: your local DPA (Norway: datatilsynet.no).

8. Your responsibility

You are responsible for having the right to store another person’s phone number and photo, and to send SMS. The app does not provide legal advice about ownership of found items.
